## Agreement workflow

1. **Choose service and dates.** Show the product, pass duration, available dates and actual inventory. Require a Basic account before collecting booking details; require Verified membership before the paid booking proceeds. Keep identity approval, mobile verification, staff roles and payment entitlements separate.
2. **Issue the server-priced quote.** Bind product/resource, dates and hours, currency, rent, deposit, tax treatment, offer and policy version to an immutable quote reference and fingerprint. Do not trust browser prices. Preserve the quoted version when a product price later changes.
3. **Confirm the contracting party.** The customer privately confirms legal name, individual/company PAN and full contracting address. A company also confirms its authorised representative and designation. Do not reuse another member's identity or infer missing values. Keep real details and agreement files outside Git, public assets, analytics and logs.
4. **Review and revise.** Show an unsigned draft and a clear Request changes action. Record requested corrections and issue a new version. Confirm the exact final version before procuring any stamp paper or sending for execution. A substantive later change requires fresh approval and signing.
5. **Complete the stamping decision.** Record whether stamping is required by the approved product policy and the confirmed agreement treatment. For a cabin, Armature pays the agreed duty. Use the owner-approved manual stamp-paper procurement route, confirming the amount, legally reviewed treatment and binding to the final document before execution. PandaDoc signs that final document. Leegality integration is not a launch dependency when this manual stamping route is reviewed and completed; its unavailability is not a stamping waiver. This is the owner's operating gate, not a claim that a particular instrument or duty is legally required without review. The current Hiwonder kit has the owner's explicit no-stamping exception; future higher-value equipment follows a separately confirmed stamping decision, including the owner's above-INR-50,000 rule.
6. **Obtain both signatures.** Send the approved document through the configured signing provider, customer first and Armature second. Notify the Armature countersigner by email when action is required. Clearly show Awaiting your signature, Awaiting Armature, and Awaiting staff review. Use separate generous signature blocks and require each signer to finish the provider session.
7. **Record staff approval.** A different Admin/Super admin account uses MFA, inspects the completed PDF and provider certificate, confirms both signers and exact quote fingerprint, and stores the restricted evidence. Record reviewer, document reference, PDF digest and approval timestamp. A typed name, uploaded unsigned draft or email assertion is not signing evidence.
8. **Take payment.** For new cabin and equipment bank-deposit quotes, verify the bank credit before enabling rent checkout. Start the 30-minute payment window when both agreement approval and deposit verification are recorded, measured from the later event. Desk payment windows remain unchanged. Recheck availability and price, acquire the inventory hold and bind the payment order to the approved quote. After expiry use a fresh availability/quote workflow; do not extend an immutable approval silently. Show the rent payable through Razorpay and the separate bank deposit clearly before Checkout opens. Razorpay's order amount must contain rent only for new bank-deposit quotes. Staff separately verify the bank credit and record receipt evidence and a unique transfer reference.
9. **Confirm fulfillment.** Verify the captured payment server-side against the issued order, amount and currency. For cabins and equipment, also require the staff-verified bank deposit before confirming fulfillment. Reserve inventory and grant the correct dated entitlement atomically and idempotently. Neither rent capture alone nor deposit verification alone may confirm a rental. Only then show confirmation, receipt and access instructions. A late capture or unresolved reservation goes to staff review, not a false success screen.

Keep the ledger independent of signing-provider-specific session URLs. Retain the approved document version, signer completion evidence and quote binding so a later provider change does not invalidate historical records. Manual review remains explicit until an equally verified automated path is implemented.

## Refrens invoicing (disabled)

Rent, desk-pass and licence-fee invoices go through a durable outbox (`private.billing_operations`, migration `202611080005`), not the browser. A database trigger enqueues one `invoice_create` and one `payment_apply` per payment, the first time Razorpay capture is recorded. Replayed callbacks and webhooks therefore cannot invoice twice. Rental invoices carry `rent_paise` only. Refundable bank security deposits are never invoiced and never recorded as revenue; they keep their own deposit ledger and receipts. Rent refunds create a `refund_review` item for staff, because Refrens documents no refund or credit-note API.

The `billing-dispatch` job sends only when both `REFRENS_INVOICING_ENABLED=true` and `private.billing_settings.invoicing_enabled` are on. Otherwise it returns a dry-run validation report with no payload values. It never sends the Refrens `email` object, so customers are not emailed. Staging fixtures are never sent by the live worker.

Duplicate protection:
- The ledger has unique keys per source and event, plus a unique invoice number `ARM-{D|R}-YYYYMM-<id>`.
- Payloads are immutable once enqueued. Claims are exclusive (`SKIP LOCKED`), and settled operations cannot change.
- Refrens idempotency is bound to one token for 24 hours, so timeouts, 5xx, 429 and in-progress replies are marked `ambiguous`.
- The next attempt pages invoices newest-first for the invoice number before resending. If the scan cannot prove the invoice is absent, the operation goes to `needs_staff`.
- Payments are checked by `refId` (the Razorpay payment id) before every apply.

GST: Armature is not GST-registered. Invoices use `invoiceType: "BOS"` (bill of supply, no tax) with no GSTIN or tax fields. `private.billing_settings` can hold a future GSTIN, state code, effective date, SAC, rate and accountant-approved treatment. A database check refuses `gst_enabled` until all of these are recorded and the state code matches the GSTIN. After that, invoices dated on or after the effective date use `invoiceType: "INVOICE"` with `billedBy.gstin`/`gstState` and item `gstRate`. A missing customer place of supply goes to staff. Refrens documents CGST/SGST/IGST only as response fields, and documents no place-of-supply or HSN/SAC input. Confirm these with Refrens and the accountant on a synthetic specimen before enabling GST.

# Armature AI Labs — purpose and positioning

Approved concept recorded 23 September 2026. This note captures the intended identity of Armature AI Labs; it is not a claim that every planned facility, device or programme is already operating.

## What Armature AI Labs is

Armature AI Labs is a makers’ lab for physical AI and robotics: a place to learn how to build intelligent hardware, test it with real equipment, and work alongside other builders. It brings together practical training, a shared prototyping lab and a community space. The long-term ambition is to bring this kind of lab to Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities across India.

## Training young builders

The lab trains people to build hardware-embedded AI products. Its intended audience includes young builders, undergraduates, graduates and hobbyists. Learning should be hands-on: people gain the ability to make, integrate and test products, rather than only study AI or robotics in theory.

## Physical AI and local compute

The planned lab equipment includes Jetson Nano devices, small GPUs, Mac minis and NVIDIA graphics cards. These resources are intended to help builders test local AI and physical AI, add intelligence to hardware, run benchmarks and prototype faster. The actual inventory and access arrangements must be confirmed before they are described as available.

## A place for builders

Armature AI Labs is also a place to co-work, collaborate, spend time together and discuss ideas. The value comes from builders learning from one another and working side by side, as well as from the equipment.

## Demos, workshops and knowledge exchange

The space is intended to host project demonstrations, training workshops and knowledge sessions. Builders should be able to present interesting work, exchange practical experience and meet others working on related problems.

## Startup support

The lab aims to help promising teams develop prototypes, move faster and receive guidance as they build startups. Incubation is part of the intended direction; do not imply a formal accelerator, funding programme or guaranteed startup outcome without a separately confirmed offer.

## Long-term footprint

The goal is to develop labs of this kind across Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities in India. Describe this as an ambition, not an existing network of open locations.

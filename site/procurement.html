02

GPU Autonomous Computer: exact GPU choices

Use consumer RTX GPUs for speed per rupee, pro GPUs only when VRAM and support matter more than price.
Recommended baseline

2 x GeForce RTX 5090 32GB

Good first Autonomous Computer build: strong CUDA ecosystem, high throughput, and enough VRAM for many robotics vision, simulation, and fine-tuning jobs. Needs serious airflow, 1000W+ class PSU headroom per NVIDIA guidance for a single card, and a motherboard/chassis planned for two thick GPUs.

Buy 2 GPUs now; add a third only after thermal validation.
Pro / quote-only

1-2 x RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell 96GB

Use when the Lab needs much larger local models, bigger batches, or pro support. The premium is huge, but 96GB VRAM changes what can run locally. Start with one quoted card, not two, unless a tenant or funded project pays for it.

Quote before PO; pricing has moved sharply in 2026.

04

Project-specific buys

These should be bought after the shared spine is in place, so hardware does not sit idle while storage, tools, and cameras are missing.
P0 arms

LeRobot + SO-ARM

Buy five two-arm leader/follower sets, print parts in-house, and buy ten spare STS3215 servos. The SO-ARM repo lists about Rs 21k for the two-arm off-the-shelf parts before printing, shipping, and tax (USD 229.88).

5 pair cells for 10 builders
Robot vacuum

Valetudo + OOMWOO

Buy two supported Valetudo vacuums from the official supported list, plus one OOMWOO prototype kit. OOMWOO currently targets about Rs 18k in sourced parts plus a Raspberry Pi 5, with the BOM still work in progress (USD 200).

2 commercial rigs + 1 DIY rig
Outdoor robot

OpenMower

Buy one donor mower, one OpenMower controller path, RTK base/rover GNSS, safety stop hardware, batteries, and outdoor barriers. Do not buy multiple mowers until the first is safe and map-aware.

1 donor mower first
Manipulation

reBot, OpenArm, hands

Buy one reBot DevArm after SO-ARM training is useful. Defer OpenArm, AmazingHand, and LEAP Hand to one pilot each because dexterous hardware needs mature cameras, arms, calibration, and safety routines.

1 medium arm, hands later
Autonomy

ArduPilot + PX4

Start with two rover chassis and Pixhawk-class controllers. Buy the drone frame only when the cage, RC failsafe, LiPo safety, and sim workflow are ready.

2 rover rigs before drones
Open infra

NetBox, OpenBMC, SONiC

NetBox runs on an existing VM. Buy a SONiC-compatible used/whitebox switch and one OpenBMC-compatible dev board only when multiple nodes and racks exist.

Track now, buy later

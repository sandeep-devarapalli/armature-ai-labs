# Armature AI Labs logo

Identity for Armature AI Labs, the maker space brand launching at HSR 1490, HSR Layout, Bangalore. This is a separate mark from the exploded-A used by the robotics lab; the two share a palette and typeface, nothing else.

## The mark

A DC motor commutator seen end-on: eight copper segments around the shaft, with the two segments under the brushes live in saffron. It is the armature of a motor, and it is eight seats around one table. The geometry lives in a 100 x 100 box and should not be redrawn: eight bars between radius 26 and 38 with corners rounded to radius 4.5, 10 degree gaps, first gap centred at 12 o'clock; the 3 o'clock and 9 o'clock segments carry the accent; shaft circle r 9.5 at the centre.

## Wordmark

"Armature AI Labs" (capital A in Armature, capital AI, capital L in Labs, rest lowercase), Space Grotesk Medium, tracking -12/1000. This replaces the earlier lowercase "armature lab" wordmark. Text is outlined to paths in every SVG so no font is needed to render them.

## Colours

- Ink `#0A1220`
- Paper `#FFFEFA`
- Cream `#F2E6CC` (segments and text on ink)
- Saffron `#E89A2C` (the two live segments only)

## Rules

- Minimum size: mark 16 px, horizontal lockup 120 px wide.
- Clear space around any lockup: the width of one segment gap times three, on all sides at minimum. Simpler: a quarter of the mark's height.
- On saffron backgrounds use the all-ink version (`-on-saffron`). Never put saffron segments on a saffron field.
- Do not rotate the mark: the live segments sit at 3 and 9 o'clock.
- Do not outline, add gradients, shadows, or change the number of segments.

# Who We Are illustration set

Original generated concept art for the selected “Growing lab” About-page direction, 23 September 2026. `approved-concept-03.png` records the selected composition. The other PNGs are the editable source exports for the site assets in `public/about/`; `who-we-are-social.png` is an opaque 1200 × 630 derivative of `lab-light.png`.

The axonometric lab is conceptual, not a drawing of a completed facility. The India map expresses a future ambition; its connections and locations are illustrative. No team likenesses or actual site plans are depicted.
